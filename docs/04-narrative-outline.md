# Narrative outline

Editorial spine for the piece. Each chapter answers a business question; charts
are evidence, not decoration.

## Opening

**Hero.** Title and standfirst over the split-adjusted NVDA price curve,
annotated at trillion-dollar closes. The curve ends on the real mid-2026
drawdown — not a monotonic triumph.

**Intro.** Thesis in continuous prose: NVIDIA's advantage is the combination of
a long software bet, a mix shift that completed before the public AI moment, and
a demand shock that arrived when the supply stack was already built.

**Industry context.** Hyperscaler capex and discrete GPU share as the market
NVIDIA was operating in — demand side before company story.

## Chapter 1 — From Graphics Company to Computing Platform

**Question.** Why spend heavily for a decade on a market that did not yet exist?

**Beat.** CUDA launch, 2006-11-08.

**Argument.** The moat is software and ecosystem cost, paid before the revenue
appeared. R&D and CUDA developer counts (as discrete, definition-labelled
datapoints) are the exhibits. Lesson: platform investments look like waste until
they look like inevitability.

## Chapter 2 — The Quiet Shift

**Question.** When did NVIDIA stop being primarily a gaming company, and did
anyone notice?

**Beat.** Pascal era, 2016-04-05.

**Argument.** Data Center revenue climbs through the late 2010s. The first
crossover vs Gaming in Q2 FY2021 is partly Mellanox and reverses for two
quarters; the permanent crossover follows. Margins rise with the mix. Lesson:
the strategic change often precedes the narrative change.

## Chapter 3 — The Demand Shock

**Question.** What did ChatGPT change in the financials — product, or demand?

**Beat.** ChatGPT public launch, 2022-11-30.

**Argument.** Product capability was already there (Hopper). What changed was
simultaneous enterprise and hyperscaler conviction. Capex, earnings reactions
and the stock reprice are the exhibits. DeepSeek (Jan 2025) shows the reverse
shock using `reactionPct`, not next-day rebound. Lesson: being early only pays
when demand finally concentrates.

## Chapter 4 — Becoming Infrastructure

**Question.** What does it mean when a chip vendor becomes a dependency of the
AI economy?

**Beat.** Blackwell announcement, 2024-03-18.

**Argument.** Record Data Center revenue, hyperscale vs ACIE diversification,
export-control volatility, $4–5T valuation, capital returns. Concentration is
both power and risk. Lesson: infrastructure businesses inherit their customers'
capex cycles and their regulators.
