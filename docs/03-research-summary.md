## Analytical turning points selected

Chosen because multiple datasets intersect, not because they are famous:

1. **CUDA platform bet (2006–2016)** — R&D intensity and developer-ecosystem evidence precede revenue.
2. **Quiet mix shift (2016–2022)** — Data Center overtaking Gaming, including the messy FY2021 false start and Mellanox.
3. **Demand shock (2022–2024)** — ChatGPT-era hyperscaler capex meets ready supply; valuation and margins reprice together.
4. **Infrastructure dependency (2024–2026)** — Blackwell/Rubin, concentration, export-control volatility, capital returns at scale.
