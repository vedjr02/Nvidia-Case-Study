## Notes

netIncome and dilutedEps are AS ORIGINALLY REPORTED. NVIDIA early-adopted ASU 2016-09 (stock-based compensation; excess tax benefits recognised in the income statement) in Q3 FY2017, applied retroactively to 1 Feb 2016. This quarter was subsequently restated to netIncome $261M and dilutedEps $0.41. Revenue, gross profit, operating expenses and operating income were unaffected. Use restated values if summing to audited FY2017 net income of $1,666M. Market-platform figures independently confirmed in 3 separate NVIDIA CFO Commentary documents.
