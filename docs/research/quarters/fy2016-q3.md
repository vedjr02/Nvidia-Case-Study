## Notes

NVIDIA's own Q1-Q4 FY2016 CFO Commentary reported only the older operating-segment split (GPU / Tegra Processor / Other); no market-platform table was published at the time. The market-platform figures here are NVIDIA's own restated market-platform disclosure for this quarter, taken from the prior-year comparison column of the corresponding FY2017 CFO Commentary (cited in sources). The FY2016 and FY2017 Form 10-K unaudited quarterly-data notes show grossProfit $733M and netIncome $247M for this quarter, vs $734M and $246M in the original Q3 FY2016 press release/10-Q used here. A $1M presentation/rounding difference; the 10-K values tie to the audited FY2016 totals.
