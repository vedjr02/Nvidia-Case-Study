# NVIDIA agrees to acquire Arm from SoftBank for $40 billion

- **Date:** 2020-09-13
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA and SoftBank Group announced a definitive agreement for NVIDIA to acquire Arm Limited in a transaction valued at $40 billion, comprising $21.5 billion in NVIDIA common stock and $12 billion in cash including $2 billion payable at signing, plus an earn-out of up to $5 billion. Closing was expected in approximately 18 months and required approvals in the UK, China, the EU and the US.

## Why it mattered

The deal would have given NVIDIA control of the CPU instruction-set architecture used across mobile and increasingly in the data centre, and would have been the largest semiconductor acquisition ever attempted. The $2 billion signing payment created downside exposure that later crystallised.

## Quantified impact

$40 billion total consideration: $21.5bn in stock (44.3 million shares), $12bn cash including $2bn at signing, plus up to $5bn earn-out.
