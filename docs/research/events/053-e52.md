# AWS makes Trainium3 UltraServers generally available

- **Date:** 2025-12-02
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

At re:Invent, AWS announced general availability of Trn3 UltraServers powered by Trainium3, its first 3nm AI chip, scaling to 144 chips per system and delivering up to 362 FP8 petaflops. AWS quotes up to 4.4x more compute, 4x greater energy efficiency and nearly 4x more memory bandwidth than Trainium2 UltraServers. AWS also disclosed that Project Rainier, built with Anthropic, connected more than 500,000 Trainium2 chips, with Anthropic expected to be on more than 1 million Trainium2 chips.

## Why it mattered

Trainium is the largest deployed non-NVIDIA training fleet by chip count and its cost claims, up to 50 per cent lower training and inference costs for named customers, apply direct price pressure at the inference layer where NVIDIA's margin is most exposed.

## Quantified impact

Up to 144 Trainium3 chips per UltraServer; 362 FP8 PFLOPs; up to 4.4x compute and 4x energy efficiency versus Trainium2; Project Rainier at more than 500,000 Trainium2 chips scaling toward 1 million.
