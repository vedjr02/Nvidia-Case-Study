# Intel cancels commercial launch of Falcon Shores

- **Date:** 2025-01-30
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

On Intel's Q4 2024 earnings call, co-CEO Michelle Johnston Holthaus said Intel would use Falcon Shores as an internal test chip only rather than bringing it to market, and would redirect resources toward a rack-scale system-level product codenamed Jaguar Shores. She acknowledged Intel was not participating meaningfully in the cloud AI data-centre market.

## Why it mattered

Intel's exit from the merchant AI accelerator market for at least one generation left AMD as the only alternative merchant supplier and removed the most plausible source of price competition in NVIDIA's core market through 2026.
