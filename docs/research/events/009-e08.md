# NVIDIA agrees to acquire Mellanox for $6.9 billion

- **Date:** 2019-03-11
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA and Mellanox announced a definitive merger agreement, signed 10 March 2019, under which NVIDIA would acquire all outstanding Mellanox shares at $125 per share in cash, an enterprise value of approximately $6.9 billion. NVIDIA said it would fund the deal from balance-sheet cash and expected it to be immediately accretive to non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP EPS and free cash flow.

## Why it mattered

Mellanox gave NVIDIA InfiniBand and high-speed Ethernet networking, which is what allows it to sell rack- and cluster-scale systems rather than discrete accelerators. Networking became a separately material revenue line, reaching $31.4 billion in fiscal 2026.

## Quantified impact

$125.00 per share in cash; approximately $6.9 billion enterprise value.
