# Commerce announces rescission and non-enforcement of the AI Diffusion Rule

- **Date:** 2025-05-13
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

The Department of Commerce announced it would rescind the AI Diffusion Rule two days before its compliance date, with Under Secretary Jeffery Kessler instructing BIS enforcement not to enforce it, on the grounds that it would stifle American innovation and downgrade dozens of countries to second-tier status. BIS said it would publish a formalising regulation and issue a replacement rule in future. The GAO later concluded the press release itself constituted a rule subject to Congressional Review Act submission requirements.

## Why it mattered

Rescission removed a worldwide licensing overhang and reopened the Middle East and sovereign-AI pipeline, but it reverted licensing to the November 2023 end-user control rather than eliminating it. The absence of a published replacement rule left NVIDIA operating under guidance rather than regulation for more than a year.
