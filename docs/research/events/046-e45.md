# NVIDIA and OpenAI sign a letter of intent for 10 gigawatts and up to $100 billion

- **Date:** 2025-09-22
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA and OpenAI announced a letter of intent for a strategic partnership to deploy at least 10 gigawatts of NVIDIA systems, representing millions of GPUs, for OpenAI's next-generation infrastructure. NVIDIA stated it intends to invest up to $100 billion in OpenAI progressively as each gigawatt is deployed. The first gigawatt is targeted for the second half of 2026 on the Vera Rubin platform.

## Why it mattered

The structure is circular by design: NVIDIA takes non-voting equity in OpenAI, which uses the cash to buy NVIDIA systems. It secures anchor demand for the Rubin generation but concentrates counterparty exposure and has drawn scrutiny over vendor financing. It is a letter of intent staged against deployment, not committed capital.

## Quantified impact

At least 10 GW of NVIDIA systems; up to $100 billion investment released progressively per gigawatt; first gigawatt targeted for H2 2026 on Vera Rubin.
