# Microsoft launches Maia 200

- **Date:** 2026-01-26
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Microsoft announced Maia 200, its second-generation custom accelerator and first built specifically for large-scale inference, on TSMC 3nm with 140 billion transistors, 216GB HBM3e, 7TB/s memory bandwidth and more than 10 petaflops of FP4. Microsoft quoted 30 per cent better performance per dollar than the current fleet generation. Initial deployments are in Iowa and Arizona, serving OpenAI production models, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Foundry. It is not available for external purchase or per-chip cloud rental.

## Why it mattered

Maia 200 is the last of the four major hyperscalers to field a production inference accelerator, completing the pattern in which every large NVIDIA customer now has an internal alternative for at least part of its inference load. Its predecessor Maia 100 never reached customer rental, so this is Microsoft's first genuine deployment at scale, years behind Google and AWS.

## Quantified impact

140 billion transistors on TSMC N3; 216GB HBM3e at 7 TB/s; >10 PFLOPS FP4; 750W TDP; clusters up to 6,144 accelerators; Microsoft-quoted 30% better performance per dollar.
