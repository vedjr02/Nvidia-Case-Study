# AMD launches Instinct MI300X

- **Date:** 2023-12-06
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

AMD announced availability of the Instinct MI300X accelerator, built on CDNA 3, alongside the MI300A APU and ROCm 6. AMD positioned MI300X on memory bandwidth and capacity for generative AI training and inference, with Dell, HPE and Lenovo systems following in 2024.

## Why it mattered

MI300X was the first credible merchant alternative to H100 and gave hyperscalers a second-source negotiating position. It did not displace NVIDIA at scale, but it established AMD as the only other vendor able to sell a full data-centre accelerator line, which matters for the 2026 OpenAI multi-gigawatt commitment.
