# Vera Rubin ramps into full production; shipments from autumn 2026

- **Date:** 2026-05-31
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

At GTC Taipei, NVIDIA announced Vera Rubin was ramping into full production, the third generation of MGX rack-scale systems, with roughly 150 supply-chain partners in Taiwan alone across more than 350 factories in 30 countries. Production shipments were set to begin in autumn 2026 to named cloud providers including AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, CoreWeave, Lambda, Nebius and Nscale, with broader OEM availability in the second half of 2026. CoreWeave was first to bring up and validate Vera Rubin NVL72 and reported a 10x improvement in tokens per second per megawatt versus Grace Blackwell NVL72 on a DeepSeek-R1 benchmark.

## Why it mattered

Full production ahead of shipment confirms NVIDIA held its annual cadence for a second consecutive generation. The tokens-per-megawatt framing is the metric that now governs purchasing, since customers are power-constrained rather than capital-constrained, and it is the basis of NVIDIA's argument against custom-silicon alternatives.

## Quantified impact

Full production from 1 June 2026; roughly 150 Taiwanese supply-chain partners across 350+ factories in 30 countries; 20.7TB of HBM4 pooled across a 72-GPU rack; CoreWeave-reported 10x tokens per second per megawatt versus Grace Blackwell NVL72 on DeepSeek-R1.
