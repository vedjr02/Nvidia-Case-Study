# Stargate announced with a stated $500 billion four-year target

- **Date:** 2025-01-21
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

OpenAI announced Stargate, a new company intending to invest $500 billion over four years in US AI infrastructure for OpenAI, with $100 billion to be deployed immediately. Equity funders were SoftBank, OpenAI, Oracle and MGX, with SoftBank holding financial responsibility and OpenAI operational responsibility. Arm, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Oracle and OpenAI were named as key initial technology partners, with the buildout starting in Texas.

## Why it mattered

Stargate named NVIDIA as a core technology partner in what was framed as the largest single AI infrastructure programme announced to date, and anchored the multi-gigawatt demand narrative NVIDIA has used since. The headline figure is an aspirational multi-year target rather than committed capital, and execution has since moved substantially to bilateral site-level deals.

## Quantified impact

$500 billion stated four-year investment intent; $100 billion initial deployment phase; five additional US data-centre sites announced 23 September 2025.
