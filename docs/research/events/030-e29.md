# SoftBank acquires Graphcore

- **Date:** 2024-07-12
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Graphcore announced it had been acquired by SoftBank Group and would continue as a wholly owned subsidiary under its own name with headquarters in Bristol. Terms were not disclosed. Graphcore had been valued at $2.77 billion at the end of 2020 but had cut headcount by a fifth to 494 employees and closed operations in Norway, Japan and South Korea after failing to secure the investment needed to compete.

## Why it mattered

Graphcore was the most prominent European attempt at a merchant NVIDIA alternative, and its distressed sale demonstrated that competing on silicon without a comparable software ecosystem and capital base is not viable. It removed a competitor and reinforced the CUDA moat argument.

## Quantified impact

Sale price undisclosed. Prior valuation $2.77bn at end-2020; headcount reduced by roughly 20% to 494 before the sale.
