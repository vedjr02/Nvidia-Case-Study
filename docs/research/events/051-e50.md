# Google makes Ironwood TPU generally available; Anthropic to access up to one million TPUs

- **Date:** 2025-11-06
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Google announced general availability of Ironwood, its seventh-generation TPU, first introduced for testing in April 2025. Ironwood offers 192GB HBM3e and 2,307 BF16 TFLOPs per chip, scales to pods of 9,216 chips, and Google quotes a 10x peak performance improvement over TPU v5p and more than 4x per chip over Trillium. Google stated Anthropic plans to access up to 1 million TPUs.

## Why it mattered

Ironwood is the most mature non-NVIDIA accelerator by deployment scale, and its availability to external customers including Anthropic, Apple, Cohere and Safe Superintelligence converts Google's internal silicon into a merchant competitor. The 9,216-chip pod domain exceeds NVIDIA's 72-GPU NVLink domain, which is a genuine architectural differentiation at very large scale.

## Quantified impact

192 GiB HBM per chip at 7,380 GBps; 9,216 chips per pod; Google-quoted 10x over TPU v5p and 4x per chip over Trillium; Anthropic access to up to 1 million TPUs.
