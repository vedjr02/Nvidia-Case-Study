# Vera Rubin platform announced at GTC with seven chips in full production

- **Date:** 2026-03-16
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

At GTC, NVIDIA announced the Vera Rubin platform with seven new chips in full production: the Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, NVLink 6 Switch, ConnectX-9 SuperNIC, BlueField-4 DPU, Spectrum-6 Ethernet switch and the newly integrated NVIDIA Groq 3 LPU. NVIDIA also introduced the DSX platform with Max-Q dynamic power provisioning, claiming 30 per cent more AI infrastructure within a fixed-power data centre. Partner availability was set for the second half of 2026.

## Why it mattered

The integration of a Groq-derived LPU three months after the licensing deal shows NVIDIA absorbing specialised inference silicon into its rack rather than leaving that workload to competitors. The power-provisioning claim addresses the binding constraint for customers, which is megawatts rather than chip availability.

## Quantified impact

Seven chips in full production; five rack configurations; NVIDIA-claimed 30% more AI infrastructure within a fixed-power data centre via DSX Max-Q.
