# Q3 fiscal 2019 miss and post-crypto channel inventory correction

- **Date:** 2018-11-15
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA reported fiscal Q3 2019 revenue of $3.18 billion against consensus of about $3.24 billion, with gaming revenue of $1.76 billion versus roughly $1.89 billion expected, and guided fiscal Q4 to $2.70 billion against consensus near $3.40 billion. Management attributed the shortfall to excess channel inventory of mid-range Pascal gaming cards left behind by the collapse of the cryptocurrency boom and said it would ship very little mid-range Pascal for one to two quarters to let the channel clear.

## Why it mattered

This was the first demonstration that crypto-linked demand could reverse violently and that NVIDIA's channel model transmits the reversal into reported revenue with a lag. It cost roughly a third of the company's equity value in two sessions and became the factual basis for later securities litigation and SEC action.

## Quantified impact

Q4 FY2019 guidance of $2.70bn versus $3.40bn consensus, a shortfall of about $700m; $57m of charges for components and chips following the crypto fall-off; shares fell 18.8% on 16 November 2018 and more than 28% over two trading days.
