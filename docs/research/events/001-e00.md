# NVIDIA unveils CUDA alongside the G80 GeForce 8800

- **Date:** 2006-11-08
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA announced CUDA, a general-purpose parallel computing architecture for its GPUs, together with the G80-based GeForce 8800. G80 was the company's first unified-shader, DirectX 10 GPU, and CUDA exposed its processor cores to developers with the industry's first C compiler for a GPU, allowing cores to communicate, synchronise and share data rather than only run graphics pipelines.

## Why it mattered

CUDA converted the GPU from a fixed-function graphics part into a programmable compute platform, creating the software lock-in that later underpinned NVIDIA's data-centre franchise. Every subsequent AI workload advantage NVIDIA holds traces back to this software layer rather than to silicon alone.

## Quantified impact

GeForce 8800 GTX launched at $599 and GeForce 8800 GTS at $449; G80 was the culmination of four years of R&D per NVIDIA.
