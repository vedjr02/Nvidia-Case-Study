# COVID-era demand surge and the onset of supply constraints

- **Date:** 2021-02-24
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA reported record fiscal 2021 revenue of $16.68 billion, up 53 per cent, with record gaming and data-centre results. The CFO commentary stated that gaming and data centre benefited from people working, learning and playing from home, and that stronger global demand had limited the availability of capacity and components throughout the supply chain, particularly in gaming.

## Why it mattered

The pandemic pulled forward several years of PC and cloud demand while simultaneously initiating the industry-wide semiconductor shortage. The resulting inability to satisfy demand set prices and channel inventory on a trajectory that reversed sharply in 2022.

## Quantified impact

FY2021 revenue $16.68bn, up 53%; gaming full-year revenue up 41%; data centre full-year revenue up 124%, of which Mellanox contributed 10% of total company revenue.
