# NVIDIA joins the Dow Jones Industrial Average, replacing Intel

- **Date:** 2024-11-08
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

S&P Dow Jones Indices announced on 1 November 2024 that NVIDIA would replace Intel in the DJIA effective before the open on 8 November 2024, stating the change was initiated to ensure more representative exposure to the semiconductor industry. Intel had been a component for 25 years.

## Why it mattered

Inclusion in a price-weighted benchmark forces index-tracking demand and is a formal marker of NVIDIA displacing Intel as the reference US semiconductor company. The June 2024 10-for-1 split was the enabling condition, since persistently high-priced stocks distort the DJIA.
