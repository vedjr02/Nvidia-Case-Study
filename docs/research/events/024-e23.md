# NVIDIA guides Q2 fiscal 2024 revenue to $11 billion, repricing the AI trade

- **Date:** 2023-05-24
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA reported fiscal Q1 2024 revenue of $7.19 billion, down 13 per cent year over year, but guided the following quarter to $11.00 billion against a consensus of roughly $7.15 billion, citing surging demand for data-centre products used to run generative AI services. Data-centre revenue was a record $4.28 billion in the quarter.

## Why it mattered

A guide more than 50 per cent above consensus is the moment the market repriced AI infrastructure demand as real and near-term rather than speculative. The share reaction added roughly $200 billion of market value in a single session and set NVIDIA on the path to a $1 trillion valuation weeks later.

## Quantified impact

Q2 FY2024 guidance $11.00bn ±2% versus roughly $7.15bn consensus; shares rose as much as 28% after hours, adding about $200bn of market value to above $950bn.
