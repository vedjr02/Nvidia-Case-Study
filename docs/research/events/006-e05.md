# Cryptomining demand materially inflates gaming GPU revenue

- **Date:** 2017-08-01
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** period (NVIDIA fiscal 2018 Q2 and Q3, quarters ended 30 July 2017 and 29 October 2017)

## What happened

As crypto-asset prices rose through 2017, buyers increasingly used GPUs marketed for gaming to mine cryptocurrency. The SEC's later order records that NVIDIA held information indicating cryptomining was a significant factor in the year-over-year growth of gaming GPU revenue during its fiscal 2018 second and third quarters, and that the company's sales personnel, particularly in China, reported significant mining-driven demand increases.

## Why it mattered

A volatile, non-recurring demand source was being reported inside a segment investors treated as structurally growing, which set up both the 2018 inventory correction and the subsequent SEC enforcement action.

## Quantified impact

None verified in the source set.
