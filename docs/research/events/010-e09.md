# Microsoft invests $1 billion in OpenAI and becomes its exclusive cloud provider

- **Date:** 2019-07-22
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** month (July 2019)

## What happened

Microsoft made a $1 billion investment in OpenAI and became the exclusive provider of cloud computing services to the lab. Microsoft has publicly confirmed the 2019 investment as the first phase of the partnership; OpenAI has confirmed the same sequence of 2019 and 2021 investments.

## Why it mattered

This was the first large corporate commitment to funding frontier-model compute, and it established the hyperscaler-plus-lab structure that later drove multi-gigawatt GPU procurement. NVIDIA was the indirect beneficiary as the underlying accelerator supplier for the Azure supercomputers built under the agreement.

## Quantified impact

$1 billion investment (2019).
