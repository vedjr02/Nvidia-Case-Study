# NVIDIA completes $700 million acquisition of Run:ai

- **Date:** 2024-12-30
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA completed its acquisition of Israeli GPU orchestration software company Run:ai, announced in April 2024, after the European Commission granted unconditional clearance in December. Run:ai provides Kubernetes-based workload management for GPU infrastructure. NVIDIA committed to open-sourcing the software, later releasing the KAI Scheduler under Apache 2.0.

## Why it mattered

Run:ai extends NVIDIA's control from silicon into the scheduling layer that determines GPU utilisation, which is the metric enterprises use to justify further purchases. The open-sourcing commitment was the concession that resolved antitrust concern about foreclosing rival accelerators.

## Quantified impact

Approximately $700 million; EU clearance granted unconditionally in December 2024 after an October 2024 review; a US DOJ inquiry was reported as ongoing at close.
