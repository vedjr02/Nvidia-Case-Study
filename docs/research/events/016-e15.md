# Arm acquisition terminated; SoftBank retains $1.25 billion prepayment

- **Date:** 2022-02-08
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA and SoftBank mutually terminated the Arm share purchase agreement, citing significant regulatory challenges preventing consummation of the transaction. Under the agreement's terms SoftBank retained the $1.25 billion NVIDIA had prepaid, NVIDIA retained its 20-year Arm licence, and Arm began preparing for an IPO.

## Why it mattered

NVIDIA recorded a $1.36 billion charge in fiscal Q1 2023 for the failed deal. Strategically it forced NVIDIA to pursue CPU capability organically through Grace and later Vera rather than by owning the architecture, and it established that large horizontal semiconductor consolidation was no longer clearable.

## Quantified impact

$1.25 billion prepayment forfeited; $1.36 billion charge recorded in Q1 fiscal 2023; 20-year Arm licence retained.
