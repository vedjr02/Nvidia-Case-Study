# NVIDIA and Meta announce a multiyear, multigenerational infrastructure partnership

- **Date:** 2026-02-17
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA announced a multiyear, multigenerational strategic partnership with Meta spanning on-premises, cloud and AI infrastructure, covering large-scale deployment of NVIDIA CPUs and millions of Blackwell and Rubin GPUs, plus Spectrum-X Ethernet switches for Meta's Facebook Open Switching System. Meta becomes the first large-scale deployer of Grace CPUs as standalone chips, with Vera CPUs targeted for 2027. Financial terms were not disclosed.

## Why it mattered

Meta had been the most credible defector, running its own MTIA silicon, buying AMD, and reportedly evaluating Google TPUs. Securing millions of GPUs plus a standalone CPU beachhead at Meta both protects a top-tier customer and opens a new product category for NVIDIA CPUs sold without an attached GPU.

## Quantified impact

Millions of Blackwell and Rubin GPUs; first large-scale standalone Grace CPU deployment; part of Meta's stated commitment to spend $600 billion in the US by 2028 on data centres and supporting infrastructure.
