# Blackwell architecture announced at GTC

- **Date:** 2024-03-18
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA announced the Blackwell platform. Blackwell GPUs carry 208 billion transistors on a custom TSMC 4NP process, using two reticle-limited dies joined by a 10 TB/s chip-to-chip link presented to software as a single GPU. The GB200 Grace Blackwell Superchip pairs two B200 GPUs with a Grace CPU, and the GB200 NVL72 liquid-cooled rack connects 36 Superchips, 72 GPUs and 36 CPUs over fifth-generation NVLink.

## Why it mattered

Blackwell moved NVIDIA's unit of sale from the accelerator to the rack, raising average selling prices by roughly an order of magnitude and pulling networking, DPUs and system integration into NVIDIA's revenue. NVIDIA claimed up to 30x LLM inference performance and up to 25x lower cost and energy versus H100 at equal GPU count.

## Quantified impact

208 billion transistors; 10 TB/s die-to-die interconnect; GB200 NVL72 claimed up to 30x inference performance and up to 25x lower cost and energy versus the same number of H100s.
