# US approves H200 sales to approved Chinese customers in exchange for a 25 per cent cut

- **Date:** 2025-12-08
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

President Trump announced that the US government would approve China sales of the H200 and equivalent products in exchange for a 25 per cent cut of the revenue. This marked a significant easing relative to the April 2025 position, moving the permitted ceiling from the purpose-built H20 up to the second-most capable Hopper part.

## Why it mattered

The decision restored a theoretical path to a market that supplied roughly a quarter of NVIDIA revenue as recently as 2022, but NVIDIA continued to exclude China data-centre compute revenue from guidance, and CEO Jensen Huang told investors in May 2026 to 'expect nothing' from Chinese sales.

## Quantified impact

25% of H200 China sale revenue payable to the US government; structure requires shipments to transit US territory because direct export fees are not permitted under US law.
