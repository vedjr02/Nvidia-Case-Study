# Microsoft, NVIDIA and Anthropic announce reciprocal investment and compute commitments

- **Date:** 2025-11-18
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Anthropic committed to purchase $30 billion of Azure compute capacity and to contract additional capacity up to one gigawatt, initially on NVIDIA Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin systems. NVIDIA committed to invest up to $10 billion and Microsoft up to $5 billion in Anthropic, and NVIDIA and Anthropic established a first-time technology partnership on co-optimisation of models and future architectures.

## Why it mattered

The deal brings the last major frontier lab that had been standardised on Google TPUs and AWS Trainium onto NVIDIA hardware, closing a competitive gap. It reportedly valued Anthropic at around $350 billion, up from $183 billion in September 2025, and further extends the pattern of NVIDIA taking equity positions in its own demand.

## Quantified impact

$30 billion Azure compute commitment plus up to 1 GW additional capacity; NVIDIA investing up to $10 billion and Microsoft up to $5 billion; Anthropic valuation reported at approximately $350 billion versus $183 billion in September 2025.
