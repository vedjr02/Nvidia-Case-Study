# NVIDIA becomes the first company to reach a $4 trillion market capitalisation

- **Date:** 2025-07-09
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA briefly crossed $4 trillion in market value in early trading on 9 July 2025, the first public company to do so. It had first reached $1 trillion in June 2023, $2 trillion in February 2024 and $3 trillion in June 2024.

## Why it mattered

The milestone marks the completion of NVIDIA's transition from a cyclical semiconductor company to the largest company in the world by market value, and made its capital allocation and supply decisions systemically relevant to equity indices.

## Quantified impact

$4 trillion market capitalisation; $1tn first reached June 2023, $2tn February 2024, $3tn June 2024; shares rose 171% in calendar 2024.
