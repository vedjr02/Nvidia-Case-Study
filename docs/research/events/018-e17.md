# SEC settles charges over inadequate cryptomining disclosure; $5.5 million penalty

- **Date:** 2022-05-06
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

The SEC announced settled charges finding that in two consecutive fiscal 2018 quarters NVIDIA failed to disclose in its Forms 10-Q, as required by Item 303 of Regulation S-K, that cryptomining was a significant element of the material year-over-year growth in gaming GPU revenue. The order found violations of Securities Act Section 17(a)(2) and (3), the Exchange Act disclosure provisions, and a failure to maintain adequate disclosure controls.

## Why it mattered

The penalty itself was immaterial to NVIDIA's finances, but the order established a documented regulatory finding on demand-source disclosure that has shaped how the company describes concentration and end-market attribution in later filings, including customer concentration and China exposure.

## Quantified impact

$5,500,000 civil money penalty; cease-and-desist order; settled without admitting or denying findings.
