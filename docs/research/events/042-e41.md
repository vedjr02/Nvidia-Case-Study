# AMD launches Instinct MI350 series and previews MI400 and Helios

- **Date:** 2025-06-12
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

AMD launched the CDNA 4-based Instinct MI350 series with 288GB HBM3E and 8TB/s bandwidth per GPU, alongside ROCm 7.0. At the same event AMD previewed the MI400 series and the Helios rack reference design for 2026, unifying EPYC Venice CPUs, MI400 GPUs and Pensando Vulcano NICs with a scale-up domain of up to 72 GPUs and 260 TB/s of scale-up bandwidth.

## Why it mattered

MI350 matched Blackwell Ultra on per-GPU memory capacity, and Helios is AMD's first attempt to compete at rack scale rather than at the accelerator level, which is the layer where NVIDIA captures the most value. Execution against the 2026 Helios timeline is the main variable in whether AMD becomes a genuine second source.

## Quantified impact

MI350X: 288GB HBM3E, 8TB/s bandwidth; AMD-claimed up to 4x generational AI compute improvement and up to 35x inference improvement; Helios: up to 72 MI400-series GPUs and 260 TB/s scale-up bandwidth, targeted at 2026.
