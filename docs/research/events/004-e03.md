# Pascal architecture and Tesla P100 introduced at GTC

- **Date:** 2016-04-05
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA introduced the Pascal architecture and the Tesla P100 data-centre accelerator, built on a 16nm FinFET process with 15.3 billion transistors, CoWoS packaging with HBM2 memory at 720GB/s, and NVLink interconnect. NVIDIA claimed over a 12x increase in neural-network training performance versus the previous Maxwell generation.

## Why it mattered

Pascal was the first NVIDIA architecture designed with deep-learning training as a first-order design target and introduced the packaging and interconnect building blocks (HBM, NVLink) that define the data-centre product line to this day.

## Quantified impact

15.3 billion transistors; 720GB/s HBM2 bandwidth; NVIDIA-claimed 12x training performance gain over Maxwell.
