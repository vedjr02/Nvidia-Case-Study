# Intel abandons its $500 million Gaudi 3 revenue target

- **Date:** 2024-11-01
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

On its Q3 2024 earnings call Intel confirmed it would not achieve its target of $500 million in Gaudi accelerator revenue for 2024, with then-CEO Pat Gelsinger citing the product transition from Gaudi 2 to Gaudi 3 and software ease-of-use. Few service providers beyond IBM Cloud committed to the part.

## Why it mattered

Intel's failure at a target representing well under one per cent of NVIDIA's contemporaneous data-centre revenue confirmed that the barrier to entry is the software stack, not the silicon. It removed the third merchant supplier from serious contention.

## Quantified impact

$500 million 2024 Gaudi revenue target withdrawn; for comparison AMD guided to roughly $3bn of AI GPU revenue for the same year.
