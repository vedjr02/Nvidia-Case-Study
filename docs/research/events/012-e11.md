# Ampere architecture and A100 enter full production

- **Date:** 2020-05-14
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA announced that the A100, the first Ampere-architecture GPU, was in full production and shipping. The A100 carried more than 54 billion transistors on a 7nm process, unified training and inference in one part, and introduced Multi-Instance GPU partitioning. The DGX A100 system with eight A100s was announced the same day.

## Why it mattered

A100 became the workhorse of the pre-ChatGPT AI build-out and the reference part against which US export controls were later written. NVIDIA quoted up to 20x performance improvement over its predecessor, which reset the cost curve for large-model training.

## Quantified impact

More than 54 billion transistors on 7nm; NVIDIA-quoted up to 20x performance gain over the prior generation; DGX A100 delivered 5 petaflops of AI performance.
