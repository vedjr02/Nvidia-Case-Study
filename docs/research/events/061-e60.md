# China falls to 9 per cent of NVIDIA revenue

- **Date:** 2026-02-25
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** period (fiscal years 2024-2026, per FY2026 Form 10-K filed 25 February 2026)

## What happened

NVIDIA's fiscal 2026 Form 10-K discloses revenue from customers headquartered in China including Hong Kong of $19,677 million on total revenue of $215,938 million, or 9.1 per cent, against $25,048 million of $130,497 million (19.2 per cent) in fiscal 2025 and $12,330 million of $60,922 million (20.2 per cent) in fiscal 2024. Revenue from customers headquartered outside the United States fell to 31 per cent of total from 41 and 48 per cent in the two prior years. Bloomberg reporting notes China supplied roughly a quarter of NVIDIA revenue as recently as 2022, with sales now below 10 per cent and mostly PC graphics rather than AI accelerators.

## Why it mattered

The structural de-weighting of China means further restriction now has limited incremental revenue effect, but it also means any reopening is upside that is not in guidance. The corollary is extreme concentration in US-headquartered customers, at 69 per cent of fiscal 2026 revenue.

## Quantified impact

China (incl. Hong Kong) revenue: FY2024 $12,330m (20.2%), FY2025 $25,048m (19.2%), FY2026 $19,677m (9.1%), all on the recast customer-headquarters basis. Non-US-headquartered revenue: 48%, 41%, 31% for FY2024-26. United States FY2026: $149,617m (69.3%).
