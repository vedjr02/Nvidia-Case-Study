# AlexNet wins ImageNet using two consumer NVIDIA GPUs

- **Date:** 2012-12-01
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** month (NeurIPS 2012 proceedings; ILSVRC-2012 competition results announced October 2012)

## What happened

Krizhevsky, Sutskever and Hinton trained an eight-layer convolutional network with 60 million parameters on the ImageNet dataset and reported a decisive improvement over prior computer-vision methods. The network was trained for five to six days on two NVIDIA GTX 580 3GB GPUs, and was split across the two cards specifically because the model would not fit in a single 3GB GPU's memory.

## Why it mattered

AlexNet established GPUs as the default substrate for deep learning and made CUDA competence a prerequisite for AI research, which is the origin of NVIDIA's structural position in AI training hardware.

## Quantified impact

60 million parameters and 650,000 neurons trained in 5-6 days on two GTX 580 3GB GPUs across roughly 90 passes over 1.2 million labelled images.
