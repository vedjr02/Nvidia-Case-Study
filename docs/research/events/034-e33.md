# BIS issues the Framework for Artificial Intelligence Diffusion

- **Date:** 2025-01-15
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

BIS published an interim final rule revising EAR controls on advanced computing integrated circuits and adding controls on the model weights of certain advanced closed-weight AI models, structured around country tiers with new licence exceptions and an updated Data Center Validated End User authorisation. The rule was effective 13 January 2025 with a general compliance date of 15 May 2025 and certain provisions delayed to 15 January 2026.

## Why it mattered

The rule would have converted advanced-accelerator export licensing from a China-specific end-user control into a worldwide destination control with per-country compute caps, exposing NVIDIA's sovereign and Middle East pipeline to licensing risk. Industry opposition was immediate.
