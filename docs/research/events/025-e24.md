# October 2023 export controls capture A800, H800, L40S and RTX 4090

- **Date:** 2023-10-17
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

BIS submitted an interim final rule amending ECCNs 3A090 and 4A090 and imposing licensing on exports to China and Country Groups D1, D4 and D5 of circuits above defined performance thresholds, explicitly including the A100, A800, H100, H800, L40, L40S and RTX 4090, and any system incorporating them. On 23 October 2023 the US government told NVIDIA the requirement for products with total processing performance of 4800 or more was effective immediately rather than after the planned 30-day period.

## Why it mattered

The rule closed the interconnect-bandwidth workaround NVIDIA had used to build compliant China parts, and shifted the licence trigger toward the buyer's headquarters. NVIDIA stated at the time that global demand strength meant no near-term meaningful financial impact, but China revenue fell from 19.2% of total in fiscal 2025 to 9.1% in fiscal 2026 on the recast basis.

## Quantified impact

Licence threshold set at total processing performance of 4800 or more for data-centre-marketed parts; accelerated effectiveness on 23 October 2023.
