# Commerce confirms only trivial H200 volumes have reached China

- **Date:** 2026-07-14
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Under Secretary of Commerce for Industry and Security Jeffery Kessler told a congressional hearing that a 'very small quantity' of H200 chips had shipped to China and Hong Kong under the licences issued during 2026, declining to give volumes or buyers but providing Congress a confidential list of applications. This was the first on-record confirmation that any H200s had cleared into China. Committee members pressed BIS to add Chinese entities to the entity list, noting no additions since October 2025.

## Why it mattered

The confirmation validates that the licensing channel functions while demonstrating that the binding constraint is now Beijing's willingness to permit imports rather than Washington's willingness to permit exports. It supports NVIDIA's decision to carry zero China data-centre compute revenue in guidance.

## Quantified impact

Volumes described only as 'trivial' and 'very few'; no figure disclosed publicly. NVIDIA continues to assume zero China data-centre compute revenue in guidance.
