# ChatGPT reaches an estimated 100 million monthly active users

- **Date:** 2023-02-01
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

A UBS study citing Similarweb data estimated ChatGPT reached 100 million monthly active users in January 2023, roughly two months after launch, making it the fastest-growing consumer application recorded to that point. TikTok took approximately nine months and Instagram roughly two and a half years to reach the same threshold.

## Why it mattered

The adoption curve is what converted AI from a projected to an urgent capital allocation question for every hyperscaler, and is the proximate cause of the 2023 GPU supply shortage that gave NVIDIA extraordinary pricing power.

## Quantified impact

Approximately 100 million monthly active users within two months; roughly 13 million unique daily visitors in January 2023, more than double December levels.
