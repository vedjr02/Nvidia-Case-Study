# Fiscal 2026 results: $215.9 billion revenue, data centre at $193.7 billion

- **Date:** 2026-02-25
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA reported fiscal Q4 2026 revenue of $68.1 billion, up 73 per cent year over year, and full-year fiscal 2026 revenue of $215.9 billion, up 65 per cent. Data-centre revenue was $62.3 billion in the quarter and $193.7 billion for the year, up 68 per cent. Management said sequential revenue growth would continue throughout calendar 2026, exceeding what was included in the $500 billion Blackwell and Rubin opportunity, with supply commitments extending into calendar 2027. Segment operating income was $139.3 billion.

## Why it mattered

Fiscal 2026 confirms the scale shift: data-centre revenue alone is roughly 13x its level at the emergence of ChatGPT, and networking has grown into a $31.4 billion business. Customer concentration also intensified, with one direct customer at 22 per cent and another at 14 per cent of total revenue, up from a maximum of 13 per cent two years earlier.

## Quantified impact

FY2026 revenue $215.94bn (+65%); data centre $193.74bn (+68%), of which compute $162.4bn and networking $31.4bn; gaming $16.0bn; segment operating income $139.3bn; Q4 revenue $68.1bn (+73%); two direct customers at 22% and 14% of total revenue; 9 GW of Blackwell deployed.
