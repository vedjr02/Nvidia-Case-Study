# AMD and OpenAI agree a 6 gigawatt deployment with a 160 million share warrant

- **Date:** 2025-10-06
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

AMD and OpenAI announced a definitive multi-year, multi-generation agreement for 6 gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs, beginning with a 1 gigawatt MI450 deployment in the second half of 2026. AMD issued OpenAI a warrant for up to 160 million AMD shares at $0.01, vesting in tranches tied to deployment milestones and to AMD share-price targets.

## Why it mattered

This is the first multi-gigawatt commitment by a frontier lab to a non-NVIDIA merchant supplier and the clearest evidence that the largest buyers intend to engineer a second source. The warrant structure transfers most of the execution risk to AMD while giving OpenAI equity upside for validating the platform.

## Quantified impact

6 GW total; initial 1 GW of MI450-series from H2 2026; warrant for up to 160 million AMD shares at $0.01 each; AMD shares rose more than 20% on announcement.
