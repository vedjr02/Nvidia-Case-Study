# DeepSeek R1 triggers a record single-day market-value loss

- **Date:** 2025-01-27
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Chinese startup DeepSeek released R1, an open-weight reasoning model claimed to match leading US models at far lower training cost, and its free assistant overtook ChatGPT in US App Store downloads. NVIDIA shares fell just under 17 per cent, erasing $593 billion of market value, the largest single-day market-capitalisation loss for any company on record per LSEG data. Semiconductor, power and AI-infrastructure companies collectively shed more than $1 trillion. NVIDIA recovered 8.9 per cent the following session.

## Why it mattered

The event was the first serious market test of the assumption that frontier AI capability requires ever-increasing compute. It did not change NVIDIA's demand trajectory, but it introduced a persistent efficiency-risk discount into the equity story and made algorithmic efficiency a standing question at every subsequent earnings call.

## Quantified impact

$593 billion of NVIDIA market value lost in one session; share price down 16.9%; Nasdaq Composite down 3.1%; Broadcom down 17.4%; more than $1 trillion of combined AI-linked market value erased.
