# NVIDIA becomes the first company to exceed a $5 trillion valuation

- **Date:** 2025-10-29
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA shares rose more than 3 per cent to close at $207.04, giving a market capitalisation of $5.03 trillion on 24.3 billion shares outstanding, the first company to cross the threshold and just under four months after first reaching $4 trillion.

## Why it mattered

The pace of the $4 trillion to $5 trillion move, compressed into under four months, both reflected the $500 billion bookings disclosure the previous day and intensified the debate over whether AI infrastructure valuations had decoupled from realised cash flows.

## Quantified impact

$5.03 trillion market capitalisation at a $207.04 close on 24.3 billion shares; stock up more than 50% in calendar 2025 to that date.
