# Turing architecture launched at SIGGRAPH

- **Date:** 2018-08-13
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA launched the Turing architecture with the Quadro RTX 8000, 6000 and 5000. Turing combined dedicated RT Cores for ray tracing with Tensor Cores for AI inference in the same GPU, which NVIDIA described as the largest change since the introduction of CUDA in 2006.

## Why it mattered

Turing extended Tensor Core availability from data-centre parts into professional and consumer GPUs, broadening the CUDA-plus-Tensor-Core installed base and giving NVIDIA an inference story outside the data centre. It also began the RTX branding that carried the gaming franchise for the next several generations.

## Quantified impact

Up to 4,608 CUDA cores per GPU; ray-tracing throughput of up to 10 GigaRays per second, quoted at up to 25x the previous Pascal generation.
