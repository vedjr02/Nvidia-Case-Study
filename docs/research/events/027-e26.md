# Google launches Gemini

- **Date:** 2023-12-06
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Google introduced Gemini, its multimodal frontier model family, in Nano, Pro and Ultra sizes, immediately upgrading Bard to Gemini Pro across more than 170 countries and making Gemini Pro available to developers via the Gemini API from 13 December 2023.

## Why it mattered

Gemini committed Google to competing at the frontier, which validated sustained multi-year training capex across all hyperscalers rather than only OpenAI's backers. Gemini is trained and served substantially on Google's own TPUs, making Google simultaneously a large NVIDIA customer and the most advanced internal alternative to NVIDIA silicon.
