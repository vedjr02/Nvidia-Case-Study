# Ten-for-one stock split takes effect and quarterly dividend reset

- **Date:** 2024-06-10
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Announced on 22 May 2024, NVIDIA effected a ten-for-one forward split by amending its certificate of incorporation, increasing authorised common stock from 8 billion to 80 billion shares. Holders of record at the close on 6 June 2024 received nine additional shares distributed after the close on 7 June 2024, with split-adjusted trading from 10 June 2024. The quarterly dividend was raised 150 per cent from $0.04 to $0.10 per share, equal to $0.01 post-split.

## Why it mattered

Beyond accessibility, the lower share price is what made NVIDIA eligible for the price-weighted Dow Jones Industrial Average five months later without distorting the index.

## Quantified impact

10-for-1 ratio; authorised shares increased from 8bn to 80bn; quarterly dividend $0.04 to $0.10 pre-split, equal to $0.01 post-split.
