# Blackwell delivers $11 billion in its first full quarter

- **Date:** 2025-02-26
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA reported fiscal Q4 2025 revenue of $39.3 billion, up 78 per cent year over year, including $11.0 billion of Blackwell architecture revenue. CFO Colette Kress described it as the fastest product ramp in the company's history and said Blackwell sales were led by large cloud service providers, which represented approximately 50 per cent of data-centre revenue. Full-year fiscal 2025 revenue was $130.5 billion, up 114 per cent.

## Why it mattered

The ramp settled outstanding questions about Blackwell manufacturing readiness and demonstrated that NVIDIA could transition an entire product generation without a revenue air pocket, while accepting low-70s gross margins during the ramp in exchange for speed.

## Quantified impact

$11.0bn Blackwell revenue in Q4 FY2025; quarterly revenue $39.3bn (+78% Y/Y); FY2025 revenue $130.5bn (+114%); large CSPs approximately 50% of data-centre revenue.
