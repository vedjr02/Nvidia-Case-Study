# OpenAI and Broadcom agree to co-develop 10 gigawatts of custom accelerators

- **Date:** 2025-10-13
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

OpenAI and Broadcom announced a collaboration under which OpenAI designs its own accelerators and systems, developed and deployed with Broadcom and scaled entirely with Broadcom Ethernet and connectivity. Rack deployment is targeted to begin in the second half of 2026 and complete by the end of 2029.

## Why it mattered

A 10 gigawatt custom-silicon programme at OpenAI, on the same timeline as the 10 gigawatt NVIDIA partnership and the 6 gigawatt AMD agreement, signals a deliberate three-way supply strategy. Broadcom's XPU model is the structural threat to NVIDIA because it lets the largest buyers convert their own workload knowledge into silicon without building a chip company.

## Quantified impact

10 GW of OpenAI-designed accelerators; deployment H2 2026 to end-2029.
