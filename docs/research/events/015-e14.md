# Four-for-one stock split takes effect

- **Date:** 2021-07-20
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Following stockholder approval on 3 June 2021 to increase authorised shares to 4 billion, NVIDIA distributed three additional shares for every share held by holders of record on 21 June 2021, after the close on 19 July 2021. Trading began on a split-adjusted basis on 20 July 2021.

## Why it mattered

The split was framed as improving accessibility for retail investors and employees receiving equity awards; it had no effect on market capitalisation but materially widened the retail shareholder base ahead of the AI re-rating.

## Quantified impact

4-for-1 ratio; authorised common stock increased to 4 billion shares.
