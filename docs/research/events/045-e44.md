# NVIDIA invests $5 billion in Intel and agrees joint product development

- **Date:** 2025-09-18
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA agreed to invest $5 billion in Intel common stock at $23.28 per share, taking roughly 4 per cent of the company, alongside a collaboration to co-develop multiple generations of custom data-centre and PC products connected by NVLink. Intel will build NVIDIA-custom x86 CPUs for NVIDIA's AI platforms and x86 SoCs integrating NVIDIA RTX GPU chiplets for PCs. The agreement does not include a foundry arrangement.

## Why it mattered

The investment secures a second, US-based x86 CPU supply path for NVIDIA's rack systems and a route into integrated gaming SoCs against AMD, while giving Intel capital and a flagship customer. It also aligns NVIDIA with the administration's domestic-manufacturing agenda at a moment when its China access depends on political goodwill.

## Quantified impact

$5 billion at $23.28 per share, approximately 4% of Intel post-issuance; Intel shares rose 23% on the announcement.
