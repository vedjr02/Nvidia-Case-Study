# Hopper architecture and H100 announced at GTC

- **Date:** 2022-03-22
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA announced the Hopper architecture and the H100 GPU, successor to Ampere, describing an order-of-magnitude performance leap over the previous generation for AI data centres. Hopper introduced the Transformer Engine and fourth-generation NVLink.

## Why it mattered

H100 became the defining compute unit of the generative-AI build-out from 2023 onward and the principal driver of NVIDIA's data-centre revenue expansion from roughly $15 billion in fiscal 2023 to $47.5 billion in fiscal 2024. It is also the part most tightly constrained by US export controls.

## Quantified impact

80 billion transistors on TSMC 4N (H100 SXM5).
