# Gaming channel inventory correction; $1.32 billion of charges pre-announced

- **Date:** 2022-08-08
- **Category:** market-shock
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA pre-announced fiscal Q2 2023 revenue of approximately $6.70 billion against a May outlook of $8.10 billion. Gaming revenue was $2.04 billion, down 44 per cent sequentially and 33 per cent year over year. The shortfall reflected lower sell-in to channel partners amid macroeconomic weakness, and NVIDIA implemented channel pricing programmes and deliberately reduced sell-in to let inventory clear ahead of a new architecture.

## Why it mattered

A $1.4 billion revenue miss inside a single quarter demonstrated how quickly the two-step gaming channel can invert. The associated inventory charges of about $1.32 billion, comprising roughly $570 million on hand and $650 million of purchase obligations, compressed gross margin severely in the quarter immediately before the data-centre business inflected.

## Quantified impact

Revenue $6.70bn versus $8.10bn outlook; gaming $2.04bn, down 44% Q/Q and 33% Y/Y; approximately $1.32bn of charges (per CFO commentary, $1.34bn total comprising $1.22bn inventory and related reserves plus $122m warranty).
