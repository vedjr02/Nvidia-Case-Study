# Meta raises 2026 capital expenditure guidance to $125-145 billion

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Category:** ecosystem
- **Precision:** month (April 2026, Meta Q1 2026 results)

## What happened

Meta reported Q1 2026 capital expenditure including finance-lease principal of $19.84 billion and raised full-year 2026 guidance to $125-145 billion from $115-135 billion, citing higher component pricing and additional data-centre costs to support future-year capacity. Mark Zuckerberg said Meta is rolling out more than one gigawatt of its own custom silicon developed with Broadcom and a significant quantity of AMD chips to complement new NVIDIA systems.

## Why it mattered

Meta's guidance raise being driven by component pricing rather than volume indicates AI hardware inflation is now a material input cost across the buyer base. Zuckerberg's framing of MTIA and AMD as complements to NVIDIA rather than replacements is the clearest statement of how the largest buyers intend to manage NVIDIA's pricing power.

## Quantified impact

2026 capex guidance raised to $125-145bn from $115-135bn; Q1 2026 capex $19.84bn; more than 1 GW of Broadcom-developed custom silicon being deployed.
