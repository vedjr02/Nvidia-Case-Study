# US imposes licence requirement on A100 and H100 exports to China

- **Date:** 2022-08-26
- **Category:** regulatory
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

The US government informed NVIDIA on 26 August 2022 that a licence would be required, effective immediately, for any future export to China (including Hong Kong) and Russia of A100 and forthcoming H100 integrated circuits, the A100X, and any system incorporating them such as DGX. The requirement extended to any future NVIDIA circuit meeting comparable peak-performance and chip-to-chip I/O thresholds. NVIDIA disclosed the requirement in an 8-K filed 31 August 2022.

## Why it mattered

This was the first time NVIDIA's flagship data-centre products were placed under destination-based licensing, converting China from an ordinary market into a policy variable. It also forced the design of China-specific derivative parts (A800, H800, later H20), a product-line cost NVIDIA has carried ever since.

## Quantified impact

Approximately $400 million of potential Q3 fiscal 2023 sales to China identified as at risk; NVIDIA shares fell about 6.5% after hours.
