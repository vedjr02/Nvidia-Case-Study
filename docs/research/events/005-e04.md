# Volta architecture and Tesla V100 introduce the first Tensor Cores

- **Date:** 2017-05-10
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA launched the Volta architecture and the Tesla V100, a 21-billion-transistor GPU containing 640 Tensor Cores, dedicated matrix-multiply-accumulate units distinct from general CUDA cores. NVIDIA quoted 120 teraflops of deep-learning performance and a 5x peak-teraflop improvement over Pascal.

## Why it mattered

Tensor Cores were the point at which NVIDIA began building AI-specific silicon rather than repurposing graphics hardware, opening a performance gap on matrix workloads that general-purpose CPUs and GPUs could not close. Every subsequent generation extends this unit.

## Quantified impact

21 billion transistors; 640 Tensor Cores; 120 TFLOPS deep-learning performance; 5x peak teraflops versus Pascal.
