# Broadcom reports $20 billion of fiscal 2025 AI revenue and a $73 billion AI backlog

- **Date:** 2025-12-11
- **Category:** competitive
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

Broadcom closed fiscal 2025 with record revenue of $64 billion, including $20 billion of AI revenue, up 65 per cent. Q4 AI semiconductor revenue rose 74 per cent to $6.5 billion as the custom accelerator business more than doubled. Broadcom disclosed AI orders on hand exceeding $73 billion to ship over 18 months, almost half of a record $162 billion consolidated backlog, added an $11 billion follow-on Anthropic order and a fifth XPU customer via a $1 billion order, and guided Q1 fiscal 2026 AI revenue to double to $8.2 billion.

## Why it mattered

Broadcom is the only competitor whose AI revenue is both large and disclosed, and its XPU model is structurally different from AMD's or Intel's: it does not sell a competing GPU but enables the largest buyers to design around NVIDIA entirely. The AI switch backlog above $10 billion also competes directly with NVIDIA's networking segment.

## Quantified impact

FY2025 AI revenue $20bn (+65%); Q4 AI semiconductor revenue $6.5bn (+74%); AI backlog above $73bn for delivery over 18 months out of $162bn total; AI switch backlog above $10bn; Q1 FY2026 AI revenue guided to $8.2bn.
