# Blackwell Ultra and GB300 NVL72 announced at GTC

- **Date:** 2025-03-18
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA announced Blackwell Ultra, comprising the GB300 NVL72 rack-scale system and HGX B300 NVL16. The B300 raises HBM3e memory to 288GB per GPU from 192GB and delivers roughly 14-15 petaflops of dense FP4. NVIDIA quoted GB300 NVL72 at 1.5x the AI performance of GB200 NVL72 and 1.1 exaflops of dense FP4, with partner availability from the second half of 2025.

## Why it mattered

Blackwell Ultra was a mid-cycle memory-and-inference refresh rather than a new architecture, aimed specifically at test-time-scaling reasoning workloads. It kept NVIDIA on an annual cadence and, per NVIDIA, raised the AI-factory revenue opportunity 50x relative to Hopper-based builds.

## Quantified impact

288GB HBM3e per GPU (from 192GB); 1.1 exaFLOPS dense FP4 per GB300 NVL72 rack; 20TB HBM3e per rack; NVIDIA-claimed 1.5x AI performance versus GB200 NVL72.
