# NVIDIA discloses $500 billion of cumulative Blackwell and Rubin bookings

- **Date:** 2025-10-28
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

At GTC Washington DC, Jensen Huang disclosed visibility into half a trillion dollars of cumulative Blackwell and early Rubin revenue over the following five quarters through 2026, explicitly excluding China and Asia. He stated 6 million Blackwell GPUs had shipped in the first four quarters of production, against 4 million Hopper GPUs over that product's entire life, and projected about 20 million units across Blackwell and early Rubin. NVIDIA also announced seven supercomputers for the US Department of Energy.

## Why it mattered

This was an unusual forward-bookings disclosure for a semiconductor company and was intended to answer AI-bubble scepticism with backlog rather than forecast. It reframed the investment debate from whether demand exists to whether supply and power can be delivered.

## Quantified impact

$500 billion cumulative Blackwell and Rubin bookings across five quarters through 2026, excluding China and Asia; 6 million Blackwell GPUs shipped in four quarters versus 4 million Hopper across its full life; approximately 5x Hopper's growth rate. Shares rose about 5% on 28 October 2025.
