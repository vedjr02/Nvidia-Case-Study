# Mellanox acquisition completes at approximately $7 billion

- **Date:** 2020-04-27
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA completed the Mellanox acquisition at a transaction value of approximately $7 billion, roughly thirteen months after announcement, following clearance in all required jurisdictions including China. Mellanox became a wholly owned subsidiary.

## Why it mattered

In fiscal 2021, the first full year of ownership, Mellanox contributed 10 per cent of total company revenue and was cited by NVIDIA as a principal driver of the 124 per cent data-centre revenue growth that year.

## Quantified impact

Approximately $7 billion transaction value at close; Mellanox contributed 10% of NVIDIA total revenue in fiscal 2021.
