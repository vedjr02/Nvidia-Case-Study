# Rubin platform unveiled with six new chips

- **Date:** 2026-01-06
- **Category:** product
- **Precision:** month (January 2026, unveiled at CES)

## What happened

NVIDIA unveiled the Rubin platform, comprising six new chips: the Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, NVLink 6 switch, ConnectX-9 SuperNIC, BlueField-4 DPU and Spectrum-6 Ethernet switch. NVIDIA stated the platform would train mixture-of-experts models with one quarter the number of GPUs and reduce inference token cost by up to 10x versus Blackwell. AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure were named as among the first to deploy Vera Rubin instances.

## Why it mattered

Rubin is the first architecture designed after the shift from training-dominated to inference-dominated demand, and the token-cost claim is the commercially relevant metric for customers whose constraint is power rather than capital. It also marks NVIDIA making the CPU, DPU and both networking fabrics co-designed parts of a single sale.

## Quantified impact

Six new chips; NVIDIA-claimed up to 10x reduction in inference token cost versus Blackwell and MoE training with one quarter the GPUs.
