# Q1 fiscal 2027: dividend raised 25x to $0.25 and $80 billion added to buyback

- **Date:** 2026-05-20
- **Category:** corporate
- **Precision:** exact

## What happened

NVIDIA reported record fiscal Q1 2027 revenue of $81.6 billion, up 85 per cent year over year, with data-centre revenue of $75.2 billion, up 92 per cent, and GAAP diluted EPS of $2.39. The board approved an additional $80.0 billion of share repurchase authorisation on 18 May 2026, without expiration, and raised the quarterly dividend from $0.01 to $0.25 per share, payable 26 June 2026 to holders of record on 4 June 2026. Q2 fiscal 2027 revenue was guided to $91.0 billion plus or minus 2 per cent, assuming no data-centre compute revenue from China.

## Why it mattered

The 25-fold dividend increase and the $80 billion authorisation mark NVIDIA's transition to returning capital at scale rather than reinvesting essentially all cash, signalling management confidence that the demand cycle is durable. NVIDIA also began transitioning to a new two-platform reporting framework in this quarter.

## Quantified impact

Q1 FY2027 revenue $81.615bn (+85% Y/Y, +20% Q/Q); data centre $75.2bn (+92%); GAAP gross margin 74.9%; net income $58.3bn; GAAP diluted EPS $2.39; approximately $20.0bn returned to shareholders in the quarter; $38.5bn remaining on the prior authorisation plus $80.0bn added; dividend raised from $0.01 to $0.25 per share; Q2 FY2027 guidance $91.0bn ±2%.
