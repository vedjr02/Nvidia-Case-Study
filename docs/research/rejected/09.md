# Rejected claim 9

## Claim

Blackwell shipments were delayed in 2024 by a packaging or mask design flaw requiring a respin.

## Reason omitted

Could not verify. The claim originated in trade and press reporting in mid-2024 and was never confirmed by NVIDIA in a filing or announcement. NVIDIA's Q4 fiscal 2025 disclosure of $11 billion of Blackwell revenue in the first full quarter is inconsistent with a material delay, and analyst notes anticipating a mid-2025 slip were explicitly addressed by that result.
