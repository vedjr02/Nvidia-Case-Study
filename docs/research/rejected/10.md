# Rejected claim 10

## Claim

BIS has published the replacement rule promised when the AI Diffusion Rule was rescinded in May 2025.

## Reason omitted

Could not verify that a replacement rule has been issued. BIS stated in May 2025 that it planned to publish a regulation formalising the rescission and would issue a replacement rule in future. As of the May 2026 BIS guidance clarifying that pre-existing November 2023 licence requirements remain in force, no replacement framework rule was identified.
