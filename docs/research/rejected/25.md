# Rejected claim 25

## Claim

Full JPR quarterly AIB market share series for 2016-2022 and 2024-Q1 2025

## Reason omitted

Not obtainable. JPR publishes only percentage-point deltas and selective levels in its free press releases; the complete quarterly share history is behind its paid AIB report ($3,000 single issue, $6,000 annual subscription). No figures were interpolated or estimated to fill these gaps.
