# Rejected claim 2

## Claim

Microsoft invested exactly $10 billion in OpenAI in January 2023.

## Reason omitted

Could not verify from a primary source. Microsoft and OpenAI both described only a 'multiyear, multibillion dollar' investment and Microsoft explicitly declined to give a figure. The $10 billion number originates with Bloomberg and Semafor reporting and has never been confirmed by either party. The event is included with the amount flagged as reported rather than disclosed.
