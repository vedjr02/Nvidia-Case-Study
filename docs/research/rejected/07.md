# Rejected claim 7

## Claim

Stargate has committed or deployed $500 billion of capital.

## Reason omitted

Overstated relative to the source. OpenAI's own announcement describes an intent to invest $500 billion over four years with $100 billion deployed immediately. Subsequent reporting indicates the joint venture entity itself had developed no data centres and that OpenAI routed activity through bilateral site-level deals. The $500 billion is an aspirational target, not committed capital, and is recorded as such in the event.
