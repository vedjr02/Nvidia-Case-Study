# Rejected claim 1

## Claim

NVIDIA acquired Groq for $20 billion in December 2025.

## Reason omitted

Could not verify as an acquisition or as a company-disclosed price. NVIDIA issued no press release and no SEC filing, and Jensen Huang stated in writing to employees that 'we are not acquiring Groq as a company'. The roughly $20 billion figure comes solely from CNBC citing Groq lead investor Alex Davis. The underlying non-exclusive licence and talent transfer is verified and included as an event; the characterisation as an acquisition and the price are not.
