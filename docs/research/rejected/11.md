# Rejected claim 11

## Claim

The 2018 post-crypto correction cost NVIDIA roughly $700 million of gaming revenue.

## Reason omitted

Not a company-disclosed figure. NVIDIA disclosed $57 million of charges for components and chips in Q3 fiscal 2019 and a Q4 guidance of $2.70 billion against $3.40 billion consensus. A sell-side estimate of roughly $600 million of mid-range Pascal channel inventory circulated at the time but is an analyst estimate, not a disclosure. The verifiable guidance gap of approximately $700 million is recorded instead.
