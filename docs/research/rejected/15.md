# Rejected claim 15

## Claim

NVIDIA's China revenue was 26 per cent of total in fiscal 2022 and 21 per cent in fiscal 2023.

## Reason omitted

Could not verify against the filings within this pass, and the two disclosure bases are not comparable. The FY2026 10-K recast prior periods onto a customer-headquarters basis only back to fiscal 2024. Bloomberg reporting supports the general statement that China supplied roughly a quarter of revenue as recently as 2022, and that is the form in which it is recorded; the specific fiscal 2022 and 2023 percentages are not asserted.
