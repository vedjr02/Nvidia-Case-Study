# Rejected claim 16

## Claim

NVIDIA had 7 million CUDA developers as of GTC 2026

## Reason omitted

No such figure was stated in the GTC 2026 keynote transcript or in Tom's Hardware's live blog of that keynote. NVIDIA appears to have stopped quoting a headline developer count at GTC 2026, emphasising installed base instead. The number is a plausible extrapolation but is not sourced, so it is excluded.
