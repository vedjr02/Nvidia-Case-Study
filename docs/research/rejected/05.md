# Rejected claim 5

## Claim

The 15 per cent H20 China revenue-share arrangement was codified into US regulation in 2025.

## Reason omitted

Actively contradicted. NVIDIA CFO Colette Kress stated on the Q2 fiscal 2026 earnings call that US government officials had expressed an expectation of receiving 15 per cent of licensed H20 revenue but that no regulation codifying such a requirement had been published, and NVIDIA disclosed litigation risk from the arrangement in its SEC filing. Only the later H200 25 per cent levy was formalised by rule.
