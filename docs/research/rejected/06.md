# Rejected claim 6

## Claim

NVIDIA holds approximately 90 per cent of the AI accelerator or data-centre GPU market.

## Reason omitted

Could not verify from a primary or auditable source. The 80-90 per cent range appears widely in secondary commentary with no consistent methodology, no defined denominator (merchant GPUs only, or including internally consumed TPUs, Trainium, MTIA and Maia), and no primary market-research citation. NVIDIA does not disclose market share in its filings.
