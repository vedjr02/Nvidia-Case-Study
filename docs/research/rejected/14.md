# Rejected claim 14

## Claim

Meta will spend $600 billion on capital expenditure in 2026.

## Reason omitted

Conflates two figures. The $600 billion is Meta's stated commitment to spend in the United States by 2028 on data centres and supporting infrastructure, cited in CNBC's coverage of the NVIDIA partnership. Meta's own Q1 2026 release guides 2026 capital expenditure including finance-lease principal to $125-145 billion, which is the figure recorded.
